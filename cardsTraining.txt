I believe we are all dealt with different cards in life, but those cards do not determine how your life will play out. 
Some people start life with a great hand, they have money, talent, support, opportunities, and luck.  Other people can be dealt with cards they never ask for. It’s easy to look at other people’s lives and just instantly assume that they just had it way easier or to even look at your own problems and think that the outcomes are already decided.
But I don’t believe life is supposed to work that way.
We can’t control every card that is handed to us, but we can always control what we do with those cards.
There have been multiple times in my life where things just didn’t go the way that I wanted them to go. There have also been times where I would sit and see someone going through the same thing and it goes so easy for them. It’s always so easy to think, “Why do they have it so easy and I don’t?”
But I realized that comparing my own cards to someone else’s cards won’t change them.
The only thing that will change is the actions I take with the cards I am given.
The outcomes of my decisions.
For me that can just be me focusing better, not slacking off, or even just taking more time on the topic. Sometimes there are just things that are outside of my control, and I just must accept it rather than sit and ponder about it.
For me that was the hardest thing to accept, that some things are just outside of my control.
But I don’t think that means effort will fix everything. Sometimes people are just given terrible cards that have no physical way of fixing, and it’s unfair to pretend that everyone has the exact same opportunities.
But I believe that for most people, comparing yourself to others is unfair not just to yourself, but to them.
There are countless people who are born rich but end up poor, and countless people that were poor one day and rich the next. The only thing you should take from those people are lessons.
But I do think that’s what is so beautiful about life. In life a royal flush can still get the same outcome as a one pair.
This affects the way I view the future, the way I view challenges in my life. I believe that if I truly put in the effort, I can achieve almost anything.
I don’t want to spend the rest of my life wishing I had been dealt with someone else’s cards.
I want to live my own life, with my own cards.
